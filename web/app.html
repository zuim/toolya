            dm-verity
            
          

          
Research tool. Replaces dm-verity with dm-linear on /system for this session only. After reboot, verity is restored.

          
DANGER: Do NOT modify /system and reboot — dm-verity mode 2 will brick the device. No known recovery.

            Eyepiece stream connect ADB first
            

              

                
                
                
                
              

              

                
                
              

              

                
                
push→latch: decoded frame handed to the display until the panel refresh that starts showing it (waiting for scan-out; 0–10 ms at 100 Hz). refresh interval: time between two new frames on the panel (10 ms = a new frame on every refresh; 20 ms steps = repeated frames).

              

              
Low-latency H.264: browser screen capture → WebCodecs hardware encoder → eyepiece_bridge on the goggles (hardware decode, direct scan-out). Needs the natartos eyepiece and overlay mods. Chromium only.

            

          

          

            Boot splash needs the splash mod
            

              
Replace the boot splash with your own image — provided by a natartos splash mod (not available yet). The stock splash lives in a partition, not a /data file, so it needs its own mod to hook.

Before you use toolya

    
toolya enables ADB and root on your DJI Goggles 3 — an unauthorized modification of your own device.

    
    
      
  • This likely violates DJI's Terms of Service and software license. DJI may suspend or ban your DJI account and/or void your warranty.
    • 
      
  • In practice this is unlikely: the change is local, there's no reliable way for DJI to detect it, and removing toolya returns the goggles to stock. But you should know the risk.
    • 
      
  • Use only on hardware you own. You accept all responsibility; toolya is as-is, with no warranty.
    • 
      
  • toolya records anonymous stats to find where rooting breaks: success/failure, OS, browser, country code, referrer domain, a hashed network id (not your IP), and a random in-browser id to de-duplicate counts — no device serial or personal data, no third-party trackers. Respects Do-Not-Track.
